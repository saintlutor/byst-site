How Standby Capacity works

Monetise the uncertainty — not just guaranteed empty space.

1

Your container is 80% full

You expect ~15% will probably remain empty at departure — but you're not certain.

2

List it as Standby, discounted

A buyer books that uncertain space cheaply, knowing it isn't guaranteed.

3a

If it ships — everyone wins

You sold space you thought would go empty. The buyer got a discount.

3b

If regular bookings fill it

The booking rolls to the next departure automatically, or refunds. No awkward conversation.
